Standard difficulty design:
- Short real-world contexts rather than bare calculations.
- Mostly one- or two-step arithmetic.
- Fractions and mixed numbers use mathematical fraction formatting.
- Comparable overall difficulty across Standard tests, while topic balance and context order can vary.
